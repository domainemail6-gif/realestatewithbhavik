Common questions
Which option is right for me?

Start from where you are. If you have one property shortlisted, take the second opinion. If you have two or more, take the shortlist audit. If you have nothing yet, take the Buyer's Brief. If you want video and drone footage of every project, and me involved from start to finish, look at Research Focus.

Why should I pay you when brokers are free?

Brokers are free because the developer pays them, which means their advice serves whoever pays. My fee comes from you, so my only incentive is that you buy right. And every fee adjusts against brokerage at closure, so in total you don't pay more anyway.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes, no charge, while I'm building out the practice. If it ever becomes unmanageable to keep it free, I'll introduce a fee for new bookings, but that won't affect a conversation already in progress.

Do you charge brokerage on top of the package?

Standard 1% brokerage applies at closure, same as anywhere else. But your advisory fee adjusts against it, so you never pay more than you would with a regular broker.

What if I don't end up buying?

The fee pays for the work delivered: research, shortlisting, site visits, negotiation time. It isn't refundable, but there's also no brokerage to pay.

How do payments work?

Directly and simply: we confirm on WhatsApp, you pay by UPI or bank transfer, you get a receipt. No gateways, no stored cards.

Which areas do you cover?

Noida, Greater Noida, Yamuna Expressway and Gurgaon as standard; the rest of NCR on request. Tier 2–3 cities via custom engagement.
