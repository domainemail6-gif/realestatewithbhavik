Why the fee is what it is

I don't advise from the sidelines. I buy the property as if it were my own.

Time is the one thing I can't make more of, and neither can you. So I keep the number of buyers I'm working with at any one time deliberately small, and I price the work to match.

The fee isn't for information. Information is cheap, and half of it is free. It's for position. Your file sits at the top of my desk. I walk the sites myself, sit in the negotiations myself, and put your purchase ahead of my own convenience, exactly as I would if I were the one signing the cheque.

It is the clearest commitment I know how to make, and it runs both ways. Someone who pays this is serious about buying well. And I have every reason to outwork anyone who might otherwise have handled this for you.

If the fee is the part that gives you pause, say so on the discovery call. I'll tell you honestly whether you're better served by a conventional broker. I'd rather turn down the work than take on a purchase I can't give this much of myself to.
